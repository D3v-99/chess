Chess Coach: Options

Hover explanations for chess.com analysis boards.

Educational use only.

This extension is for educational analysis only. Using it in rated games violates chess.com's Terms of Service. It never plays moves for you, and it only activates on the analysis board (and, if you opt in below, on finished games). It stays off on play, puzzle and tournament pages, and whenever a game clock is running.

General

Engine (Stockfish 19 lite, runs locally)

Each hover runs up to two searches (the position, then your candidate move). The search stops at whichever limit it reaches first. Results are cached.
